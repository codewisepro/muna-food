MUNA FOOD WEBSITE
- index.html: website content and responsive navigation.
- styles.css: styling and mobile/tablet layouts.

Preview by opening index.html in a browser. Internet access is required for the remote food photos.

Publish by uploading BOTH index.html and styles.css to your hosting provider's web root (often public_html, www or htdocs), then connect your domain and enable HTTPS.

Before publishing, replace sample menu items/prices, phone number, address, opening hours and email with verified business details. Review the remote images and replace them with photos you own or are licensed to use.
